Club History

Real Madrid, founded in 1902, is one of the most successful and iconic football clubs in the world. With a rich history of triumphs, the club has set records in domestic and international competitions, earning a reputation for excellence and dominance.

With a legacy built on legendary players, tactical brilliance, and an unwavering commitment to success, Real Madrid continues to inspire millions of fans worldwide. Join us as we pursue even greater achievements on and off the pitch!
